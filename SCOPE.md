# Scope: Hebrew AI Models

Part of the [Hebrew AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI) index. This list covers **models** that improve support for modern Hebrew across AI tasks.

## In scope

- Hebrew-specialist models: trained, fine-tuned or adapted for Hebrew, from LLM adaptations to small specialist models (nikud, morphology, punctuation). Includes quantisations and format conversions of those models.
- English-Hebrew code-switching models and he↔en translation models.
- Notable multilingual models with real Hebrew support, in a separate *Multilingual models with Hebrew support* subsection, never mixed with the specialist subsections.
- Tasks: LLMs, speech recognition, text-to-speech, OCR and handwriting, NLP, embeddings, translation, summarisation.

The specialist/multilingual test is in [docs/classification.md](docs/classification.md).

## Out of scope

- **Israeli AI in general.** Israel is the only country where modern Hebrew is an official language, so it is the natural centre of activity, and many entries here are Israeli. But an AI company, project or person that just happens to be based in Israel is out of scope: that list is far too large, and this set is about Hebrew-language AI specifically. Israel-focused AI is indexed separately in [Israeli-AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI).
- Multilingual models that merely list Hebrew among many languages, with nothing Hebrew-specific to say about them.
- Datasets ([Hebrew-Datasets](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-Datasets)) and people/organisations ([Hebrew-AI-Community](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI-Community)).
